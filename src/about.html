About us

Austin Area Firewise Alliance

A volunteer organization working on wildfire preparedness with neighborhoods across Travis County — inside the City of Austin and throughout the unincorporated county.

What we are

The Alliance is residents. We are not a fire department, a city office, or a county agency, and we have no authority to order anything. What we have is the part of wildfire preparedness that has always belonged to neighborhoods: clearing the fuel around our own houses, organizing the streets we live on, and asking the agencies clear questions about what happens on the day.

Fire departments across the county have spent years building this knowledge and sharing it with residents. Carrying that work forward into a resident-run organization is the point of the Alliance: the departments have a fire to fight, and the preparation is ours to keep doing.

We help neighborhoods organize

Through the Firewise USA® steps — risk assessment, action plan, annual investment, renewal — and past the places new groups usually stall.

We connect residents to the right agency

Whether your address is covered by the Austin Fire Department or by an Emergency Services District changes who assesses your property and who partners on an application.

We press on evacuation planning

Especially for neighborhoods with a single exit. We put the specific questions to the agencies and publish what comes back.

We pass along what works

Chipper days, brush disposal, getting an assessment scheduled, keeping a committee staffed. Solved once should mean solved for everyone.

Where we work

All of Travis County. The wildland-urban interface here is not a band around the edge of the city — it runs through it, along every greenbelt, canyon, and cedar break, and out into the county on both sides of the river. A fire does not care which jurisdiction it started in, and neither does the preparation.

If your neighborhood is in a surrounding county and wants to organize, get in touch anyway. We would rather point you at the right contact than have you stop looking.

Where our guidance comes from

Nothing on this site is invented. It follows established national and state practice, and the sources are worth going to directly.

NFPA / Firewise USA®

The recognition program and the research behind defensible space and home ignition zones. nfpa.org/firewise

Texas A&M Forest Service

The state wildfire agency: risk assessments, the Texas Wildfire Risk Assessment Portal, and statewide incident information. tfsweb.tamu.edu

Local fire departments and ESDs

The Austin Fire Department and the county's Emergency Services Districts — the people who assess properties and respond.

Regional emergency management

Warn Central Texas for alerts, the Travis County Fire Marshal for burn bans, TDEM for the STEAR registry.
