## Most people add themselves
- section: members
- target: #member-list

Almost everyone on this list put themselves on it, through the form on the home
page. You do not have to do anything for that to work.

This screen is for looking after the list, and for the occasional person who
signed up on paper at a meeting.

## Nobody is on the list until they confirm
- section: members
- target: #member-counts

Filling in the form is not enough on its own. We send one message asking them
to confirm, and nothing else is ever sent until they answer it.

That is a legal requirement, and it is also what keeps our messages out of
spam folders. Somebody who has not confirmed shows as Pending and receives
nothing.

## What the statuses mean
- section: members
- target: #member-search

Confirmed means they answered and they receive messages. Pending means they
have not, and they get nothing — if they say it never arrived, use Resend and
tell them to look in spam.

Unsubscribed means they asked to stop. Bounced means the address does not
exist. Neither is ever sent to again.

## Adding somebody yourself
- section: members
- target: #member-new

Only for somebody who actually asked — a paper sign-up sheet, or a request in
person.

The form makes you write down how you got their consent, and that sentence is
stored with their record as the evidence. They still get the confirmation
message. Nobody can be marked confirmed by hand.

## Never add an address from a list
- section: members

Not from a neighbourhood directory, not from another organisation's roster, not
from a spreadsheet somebody sent you.

That is what the law calls an imported list, and one complaint from it can cost
the Alliance the ability to send anything at all. There is no import button in
this software, on purpose.

## Unsubscribe or delete
- section: members
- target: #member-export

If somebody asks to stop, unsubscribe them. Messages stop immediately and the
record stays, so a later form submission cannot quietly put them back on.

Delete only if they ask to be erased entirely — it removes the consent evidence
with them. Download CSV gives you the whole list; it is personal information,
so keep it off shared drives.
