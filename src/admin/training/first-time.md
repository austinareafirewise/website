## Signing in
- section: content
- target: #who-email

You get to the editing area by adding `/admin/` to the end of the website
address, and signing in with your own email and password.

Every board member has their own. There is no shared password, and nobody else
needs to know yours.

## If you have forgotten your password
- section: content

On the sign-in page there is a link under the password box that says "Forgot
your password?". Use it. A code is emailed to you and you choose a new one.

Nobody has to do that for you. The code comes from an Amazon address rather
than from us, so if it does not arrive within a minute, look in your spam
folder.

## The five sections
- section: content
- target: #admin-nav

The tabs across the top are the five things you can look after.

News and notices is what visitors read. Members is everyone who asked to hear
from us. Messages is what we send them. Firewise groups is the neighborhood
groups. Board is the roster of who serves.

## Help is on every screen
- section: content
- target: #help-open

The question mark at the end of the tabs opens the help for whichever section
you are in. It is written in plain words and it is always about what is in
front of you.

Once it is open you can read any other topic. The one worth ten minutes, once,
is "How the site works".

## Nothing you do here is dangerous
- section: content
- target: #new-notice

You cannot break the website from this screen.

Nothing a visitor sees changes until you press Publish, a draft is invisible to
everyone but the board, and anything published can be taken straight back off.
If a save fails, nothing was changed — it is safe to stop and ask.
