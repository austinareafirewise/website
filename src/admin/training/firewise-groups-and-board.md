## The groups list is public, in one place
- section: groups
- target: #group-new
- audio: firewise-groups-and-board-01-d8d3f1.mp3
- audio-hash: 1b305c56

Firewise groups and the board roster. Two lists in this lesson: the
neighborhood groups the Alliance works with, and the record of who serves on
the board.

These are the neighborhood groups. This list is also the dropdown people
choose from on the sign-up form, so the names are read by residents.

Nothing else about a group is shown on the website — not the area, not the
leadership.

## Status decides what happens
- section: groups
- screen: editor
- open: #group-new
- target: #f_status
- audio: firewise-groups-and-board-02-7f0b0b.mp3
- audio-hash: 762ed99e

This is the group's Status, and it decides more than it looks. The choices are
prospective, in progress, recognized, lapsed, and inactive.

Inactive hides the group from the sign-up form. Use that rather than deleting a
group that has stopped meeting — deleting loses its history, and the people who
chose it keep their records but lose the group.

## Describe the area as a resident would
- section: groups
- screen: editor
- open: #group-new
- target: #f_area
- audio: firewise-groups-and-board-03-f8998f.mp3
- audio-hash: b42f3fd7

This box is "Area it covers". It is where you say which part of town the group
is, in the words a resident would use — streets, a subdivision, "everything
north of the creek". Not a formal boundary.

This is the line somebody reads to work out whether the group is theirs, so
write it for them rather than for the paperwork.

## The board roster is not a set of logins
- section: board
- target: #board-note
- audio: firewise-groups-and-board-04-80ad10.mp3
- audio-hash: 4ea86f30

This is who serves on the board. It is deliberately separate from who can sign
in to this website.

Adding somebody here does not give them access, and removing them does not take
it away. That keeps the roster safe for anyone to correct.

## When somebody leaves the board
- section: board
- screen: editor
- open: #person-new
- target: #f_status
- audio: firewise-groups-and-board-05-db9d59.mp3
- audio-hash: 1402d56c

Somebody leaving the board is two separate jobs, and doing one without the
other is worse than doing neither — the roster then says something untrue.

The first job is on this form. You reach it with Edit on that person's row.
Set Status, the box outlined here, to "Past board member", and fill in Term
ended just below it.

The second job is not on this screen at all. Ask whoever manages the AWS
account to remove their sign-in. The roster and the logins are kept separate on
purpose, so neither one removes the other.
