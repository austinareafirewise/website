## The Alliance's board, and nobody else
- section: board
- target: #board-note
- audio: alliance-board-roster-01-a7a6cd.mp3
- audio-hash: 849ab389

The Alliance board roster. This tab is who serves on the board of directors of
the Austin Area Firewise Alliance.

It is not where a neighborhood group's officers go. A Firewise group has its
own chair and its own contacts, and those are entered on that group's record
under Firewise groups. Somebody can appear in both lists, but a group's chair
does not become an Alliance director by being typed in here.

## A record here is not a login
- section: board
- target: #board-note
- audio: alliance-board-roster-02-0705c9.mp3
- audio-hash: f06d5ef9

The roster is deliberately separate from who can sign in to this website.

Adding somebody here does not give them access, and removing them does not take
it away. That is what keeps the roster safe for anyone to correct: you cannot
lock somebody out, or let somebody in, by fixing a name.

## When somebody leaves the board
- section: board
- screen: editor
- open: #person-new
- target: #f_status
- audio: alliance-board-roster-03-10c1bc.mp3
- audio-hash: 1402d56c

Somebody leaving the board is two separate jobs, and doing one without the
other is worse than doing neither — the roster then says something untrue.

The first job is on this form. You reach it with Edit on that person's row. Set
Status, the box outlined here, to "Past board member", and fill in Term ended
just below it.

The second job is not on this screen at all. Ask whoever manages the AWS
account to remove their sign-in. The roster and the logins are kept separate on
purpose, so neither one removes the other.
