## An article, not a notice
- section: content
- target: #new-news
- audio: write-a-news-article-01-ae1ab1.mp3
- audio-hash: 663eb855

A news article gets a page of its own, with room for pictures and as many
words as it needs. A notice is a band at the top of the home page for
something happening on a date.

If it has a date and a place, it is probably a notice. If it is something to
read, it is an article.

## Write it
- section: content
- screen: editor
- target: #f-body
- audio: write-a-news-article-02-9f2328.mp3
- audio-hash: 1a6c6252

Title, then the text. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

If you want headings, bold text or bullet points, tick the Markdown box and use
the "What is Markdown?" button beside it — it shows what to type next to what
visitors will see.

## Put it on the home page as well
- section: content
- screen: editor
- target: #wrap-home
- audio: write-a-news-article-03-9b125a.mp3
- audio-hash: d9b9ea6e

Ticking this puts the article's title in the News section of the home page,
under the headline.

It is there to draw attention, so use it for the ones that matter. If every
article is on the home page, none of them stands out.

## Pictures
- section: content
- screen: editor
- target: #dropzone
- audio: write-a-news-article-04-424f2a.mp3
- audio-hash: 000f4c37

An article takes up to three. Drop one on the dotted box and drag the frame to
choose what shows.

Every picture needs a sentence saying what is in it. That is what somebody
using a screen reader hears instead of seeing it, so it is required rather than
optional.

## Where each picture sits
- section: content
- screen: editor
- target: #pictures
- audio: write-a-news-article-05-46d0ce.mp3
- audio-hash: 3ba1440e

Each picture is either across the whole width, or beside the text on the left
or the right with the words flowing round it.

They spread down the article in the order you add them. You can change where
one sits at any time from its card, and "Rotate / re-crop" turns a photo that
came out on its side.

## Preview, then publish
- section: content
- screen: editor
- target: #preview
- audio: write-a-news-article-06-b31d03.mp3
- audio-hash: ba4cd413

Preview opens the real News page with your article in it. Use it every time.

Then Publish, and allow up to a minute. Both dates are required to publish —
the article appears and disappears on its own between them.
