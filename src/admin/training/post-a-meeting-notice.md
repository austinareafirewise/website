## What a notice is
- section: content
- target: #new-notice
- audio: post-a-meeting-notice-01-2e6a09.mp3
- audio-hash: 81058a9c

Post a meeting notice. This lesson takes you through getting a meeting, a
seminar or a chipper day onto the top of the home page.

A meeting notice is the band across the top of the home page, above the big
headline. It is for something happening on a date.

Press "New meeting notice" to start one.

## The title and the words
- section: content
- screen: editor
- open: #new-notice
- target: #f-title
- audio: post-a-meeting-notice-02-af391d.mp3
- audio-hash: 881e5ced

Give it a title somebody can understand at a glance, then the details
underneath.

Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Keep it short — this sits above
everything else on the home page, so a notice the length of an article pushes
the rest of the page down.

## What kind of notice it is
- section: content
- screen: editor
- open: #new-notice
- target: #wrap-tone
- audio: post-a-meeting-notice-03-385f55.mp3
- audio-hash: 8b9db79c

Choose what the notice is about. A meeting, an event, something that changed,
or good news.

You pick the kind and the site picks the color and the little label above the
title. Use "Please read" sparingly — it is the loudest one, and it stops being
loud if everything uses it.

## When the meeting actually is
- section: content
- screen: editor
- open: #new-notice
- target: #f-event-start
- audio: post-a-meeting-notice-04-38ee6a.mp3
- audio-hash: 8f5e0829

These boxes are the date and time of the thing itself — the meeting, the
chipper day. This is what the notice prints for people to read.

It is separate from the two boxes below, and that separation matters: you want
the notice on the site weeks before the meeting, and you want it to say the
real date of the meeting. One box could not do both. There is an end time too,
if you want to say when it finishes.

Leave it blank if the notice is not about something happening at a set time.

## When the notice appears, and when it goes
- section: content
- screen: editor
- open: #new-notice
- target: #f-starts
- audio: post-a-meeting-notice-05-0037a7.mp3
- audio-hash: d0106fc3

These two are about the notice, not the meeting. Both are required, both are
Central time.

The notice appears on its own at the first time and disappears on its own at
the second, so nobody has to remember to take it down. Show it from as soon as
you have written it, and stop it just after the event.

## A picture, if you have one
- section: content
- screen: editor
- open: #new-notice
- target: #dropzone
- audio: post-a-meeting-notice-05-d4b218.mp3
- audio-hash: b93bb66f

A notice can carry one picture, drawn small beside the words.

Drop a photo on the dotted box, drag the frame to choose what shows, and say
what is in the picture — that sentence is what somebody using a screen reader
hears instead of seeing it, which is why it is required.

## Look at it before anybody else does
- section: content
- screen: editor
- open: #new-notice
- target: #preview
- audio: post-a-meeting-notice-07-68e828.mp3
- audio-hash: e4267685

Preview opens the real home page with your notice on it. Not an approximation
— the actual page, with your wording, your picture and your layout.

It shows the notice whatever its dates say, so it is not a check that the
notice will be live. What it does show is the date printed on the notice —
the "When is it?" time — so this is where you catch having typed the wrong day
for the meeting.

## Publish
- section: content
- screen: editor
- open: #new-notice
- target: #publish
- audio: post-a-meeting-notice-07-11cdd6.mp3
- audio-hash: 3e6beb2d

Publish puts it on the site. Allow up to a minute for it to appear.

If the start time has not arrived yet, it will say "Scheduled" and appear by
itself when the time comes.

There is also "Take off the site", which turns a published notice back into a
draft without deleting anything. You will not see that button yet — it only
appears once something has actually been published.
